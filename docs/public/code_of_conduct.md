# Brain Wave Collective - Code of Conduct

## Our Approach

We're building a collaborative community where everyone can contribute effectively. Here's what we expect from all members.

## The Basics

**Be kind.** Treat people well, listen to different perspectives, and give credit where it's due.

**Communicate clearly.** Ask questions, share knowledge, and offer helpful feedback. We're all here to learn and grow.

**Work together well.** Support each other's success, follow through on what you commit to, and respect people's time and boundaries.

**Keep it professional.** Respect confidentiality, honor intellectual property, and represent our community well.

**Accept people's choices.** If someone says they don't want to work on something or with someone, that's their call. No drama needed—just respect it and move on.

## What Doesn't Fly

We don't tolerate harassment, personal attacks, sharing confidential info without permission, or behavior that makes people uncomfortable. If you're making the community worse instead of better, we'll address it.

## When Things Go Sideways

Got an issue? Talk directly with the person if it feels safe and appropriate. For bigger problems or when that won't work, contact [conduct@brainwavecollective.ai](mailto:conduct@brainwavecollective.ai). We'll handle things fairly and keep the community healthy.

## Questions?

Not sure about something? Reach out to [conduct@brainwavecollective.ai](mailto:conduct@brainwavecollective.ai).

## Updates

This code of conduct may be updated from time to time. It's your responsibility as a member to stay current with the latest version.
