# Membership at Brain Wave Collective

Welcome to the Brain Wave Collective! We offer multiple membership paths designed to fit your preferred level of involvement, each with distinct benefits and responsibilities.

[Join the Brain Wave Collective →](https://brainwavecollective.ai/join)

---

## Our Membership Structure

### [Community Member](./community)
**Basic Access**  
Engage with our collective through knowledge-sharing and networking while maintaining complete independence.

### [Contributing Member](./contributing) 
**Core Participation**  
Drive collective value creation through sustained contributions while enjoying exclusive resource access and enhanced patronage allocations.

### [Investor Member](./investor)
**Capital Support**  
Provide financial investment to fuel cooperative growth while maintaining rights to returns and strategic participation.

---

*All membership types start as community members. Members may hold multiple classifications simultaneously (e.g., both Contributor and Investor).*

Our membership framework embodies our core values of collaboration, shared resources, democratic control, and innovation acceleration—creating an ecosystem where individual independence and collective progress thrive together.
