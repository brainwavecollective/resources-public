# Membership at Brain Wave Collective

Welcome to the Brain Wave Collective! We offer multiple membership paths designed to fit your preferred level of involvement, each with distinct benefits and responsibilities.

## Our Membership Structure

### [Community Member](/docs/public/community)
**The Foundation**  
Access knowledge-sharing and networking opportunities while maintaining complete independence.
[Join as a Community Member →](https://brainwavecollective.ai/join)

### [Contributing Member](/docs/public/contributor) 
**Active Participation**  
Create collective value through sustained contributions while enjoying enhanced resource access and potential patronage allocations.

### [Investor Member](/docs/public/investor)
**Capital Support**  
Provide financial investment to fuel cooperative growth while maintaining rights to returns and strategic governance participation.

---

*All members will be community members. Members may hold multiple classifications simultaneously (e.g., both Contributor and Investor).*

Our membership framework embodies our core values of collaboration, shared resources, democratic control, and innovation acceleration—creating an ecosystem where individual independence and collective progress thrive together.
