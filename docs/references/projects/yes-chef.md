![Yes Chef!](/img/yes-chef.jpeg)

In an unlikely confluence of VoiceAI ([Cartesia](https://cartesia.ai/?utm_source=brainwavecollective.ai), [Pipecat](https://docs.pipecat.ai/getting-started/introduction?utm_source=brainwavecollective.ai), [PicoVoice](https://picovoice.ai/platform/porcupine/?utm_source=brainwavecollective.ai), ), the [LeRobot](/docs/references/projects/lerobot) robotic arm, and a puppet, three friends built a project that has led to curiosity, learning, and many laughs.  

The project pulls imagery from a camera at request of a wake word, processes that imagery using an LLM, and converts related text to speech. All processing occurs on a Rasbperry Pi.

> ⚠️ **WARNING**  
> The demonstration for this project is vulgar, foul-mouthed, and relentlessly hurls insults that are inappropriate for most audiences.

You can view our [Yes Chef! hackathon submission video](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/J3BygZpa6Eo?utm_source=brainwavecollective.ai) on YouTube.

We won second place at the related competition in October of 2024.
