# Eye see you

<img src="/resources/img/monster-eye.jpg" alt="October 13th 2026" width="33%" style={{marginBottom: '24px'}} /> 

Reachy Mini has joined the eyecult, and now you can too! Turn on face tracking and let it run. No conversation or other interaction required.

## Printing the Monster Eye

<a href="/resources/assets/ReachyMiniMonsterEye_DanielRitchie_v1.0.stl" target="_blank">Download the STL</a>

This part replaces your existing face plate. It is designed to print "face up." Don't forget to paint out supports to omit the screw holes or else you are going to have a bad time. There are lots of ways this can be improved, but have found 0.2mm speed and snug supports to work well enough. [Email me](mailto:daniel@clankworks.ai) if you are having any trouble.

## Painting

There are a ton of directions you can go with this. Keep it white for a classic Reachy aesthetic, or paint it with a color of your choice. Styles range from 'normal' eyeballs to cat eyes or monster eyes... the limit is your imagination.

## Installation 

Remove your existing face plate and camera. Disconnect your camera and transfer it to this new one after it's painted, making sure that your lens has adequate clearance before tightening screws).
