## That's one creepy baby

<img src="/resources/img/creepy-baby.jpg" alt="October 13th 2026" width="33%" style={{marginBottom: '24px'}} /> 

This aberration was made for a Halloween Party for one of my friends in Denver. It's based on a modified Reachy Mini robot (Pollen Robotics/Hugging Face/NVIDIA) and runs a fairly standard speech-to-speech pipeline, except that the voice digitally altered to sound like a creepy baby. One of it's eyes is a camera, which it uses to track faces and keep an eye on you (the image is only used for identifying the location of your face; no biometrics are captured or utilized). The glitchy glowing eye is provided by a [Reachy Eye](https://brainwavecollective.ai/resources/docs/references/projects/reachy-eyes/) modified to fit the baby head.
